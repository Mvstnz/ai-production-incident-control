# SOP-MACHINE-BREAKDOWN-v1

Intersect the verified outage with scheduled operations and capacity. Check an alternative's capability, site and free interval. Show the original machine schedule separately from a proposal: the proposal reserves no capacity.

An internal ticket may be automatic. A production manager must approve exact operation, machine, hours and start/end before any synthetic reschedule. The adapter revalidates capacity at execution. A notice or reschedule proposal does not prove restored machinery. No equipment controls are exposed.
