# SOP-QUALITY-ISSUE-v1

Verify inspection and lot identifiers, material, inspected quantity, failed result and provenance in ERP. Follow lot allocations to shipment items. A verified defective lot in a pending shipment forces CRITICAL. Unverified claims enter urgent manual review.

Only a quality manager may approve the exact synthetic shipment block, including lot, inspection, quantity and shipment-item IDs. Internal escalation may occur while approval is pending. No automatic block or release is permitted. Release needs a separate quality disposition with evidence.

Already shipped quantities require manager review and escalation. The system does not claim to perform a recall or replace safety systems.
