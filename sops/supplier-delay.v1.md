# SOP-SUPPLIER-DELAY-v1

Use one verified purchase-order position and a consistent ERP snapshot. Compare the original confirmed schedule with its replacement. An offered partial delivery remains hypothetical until confirmed; a split replaces final-arrival quantity.

Record an internal ticket. Draft a request to the allowlisted synthetic supplier. The purchasing role approves the exact email; a CRITICAL plan requires the production manager. Approval covers the immutable revision, assessment, recipient, subject and full text. Do not regenerate text after approval.

After sending, monitor confirmed material availability. A delivered email does not resolve the incident. Resolution requires verified receipt and covered demand. No purchase, real ERP command or customer commitment is permitted.
